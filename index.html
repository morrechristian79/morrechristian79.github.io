Our Projects

Welding Works and Boiler Repair

Welding works and boiler repair involve repairing damaged, worn, or defective boiler parts such as pipes, tubes, pressure vessels, drums, and structural components. The work may include cutting, fabrication, replacement of parts, crack repairs, leak sealing, and reinforcement welding to restore the boiler's safe and efficient operation. These activities are performed in accordance with applicable safety standards and industry regulations to ensure equipment reliability, operational efficiency, and compliance.

Structural and Fabrication Works

Structural and fabrication works include the cutting, bending, welding, and assembly of metal materials to create frameworks, supports, platforms, railings, staircases, equipment bases, and other structural components. These activities ensure that structures are built to meet design specifications, safety requirements, and operational needs.





Mechanical Works

Mechanical works involve activities related to the assembly, operation, and upkeep of mechanical systems to ensure their safe, efficient, and reliable performance. These works may include equipment installation, piping fabrication and installation, alignment, maintenance, troubleshooting, and replacement of mechanical components.
